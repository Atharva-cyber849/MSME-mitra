GST Registration Guide for MSMEs

Complete Step-by-Step Guide to GST Registration and Compliance

What is GST?
Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive indirect tax levied on manufacture, sale, and consumption of goods and services at national level. It replaced multiple indirect taxes like VAT, service tax, excise duty, etc.

Implementation Date: July 1, 2017
Administered by: GST Council
Government Portal: www.gst.gov.in

Types of GST:

1. CGST (Central GST): Collected by Central Government on intra-state sales
2. SGST (State GST): Collected by State Government on intra-state sales
3. IGST (Integrated GST): Collected by Central Government on inter-state sales and imports
4. UTGST (Union Territory GST): Applicable in Union Territories

GST Rates in India (2026):

0% GST:
- Fresh fruits, vegetables, milk, eggs
- Bread, salt, curd, lassi
- Educational services
- Healthcare services
- Books, newspapers
- Agricultural products (unprocessed)

5% GST:
- Processed food items
- Medicines and drugs
- Transport services (rail, air economy)
- Small restaurants (non-AC)
- Footwear below ₹500
- Apparel below ₹1,000
- Domestic LPG, coal

12% GST:
- Computers, processed food
- Butter, ghee, dry fruits
- Mobile phones
- Apparel above ₹1,000
- Business class air travel
- State-run lotteries
- Hotels (below ₹7,500/night)

18% GST:
- Most goods and services
- IT services
- Telecom services
- Restaurants (AC)
- Branded garments
- Capital goods
- Industrial intermediaries
- Hotels (₹7,500+ per night)

28% GST:
- Luxury items
- Consumer durables (AC, refrigerator)
- Automobiles
- Aerated drinks
- Tobacco products
- Cement, paints
- Private-run lotteries

Who Must Register for GST?

Mandatory Registration:

1. Turnover-Based:
   - Annual turnover > ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for special category states)
   - For service providers: > ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs for special category states)
   - E-commerce sellers/operators (no threshold)

2. Interstate Supply:
   - Any interstate supply of goods/services (even if turnover < ₹40 lakhs)
   - Casual taxable persons
   - Non-resident taxable persons

3. Specific Categories:
   - Input Service Distributor
   - Reverse charge mechanism applicable
   - E-commerce aggregator
   - Online information database access or retrieval services
   - Agents of supplier
   - TDS/TCS deductors

Voluntary Registration:
- Even if turnover below threshold
- To avail input tax credit
- Business credibility
- Inter-state supplies
- Easier compliance

Who is Exempt:
- Pure agricultural activities (cultivation, harvesting)
- Turnover below threshold (and no interstate supply)
- Professionals under composition scheme
- Handicraft goods sellers (with exemption certificate)

Special Category States (₹20 lakhs/₹10 lakhs threshold):
- Arunachal Pradesh
- Assam
- Jammu & Kashmir
- Manipur
- Meghalaya
- Mizoram
- Nagaland
- Sikkim
- Tripura
- Himachal Pradesh
- Uttarakhand

GST Registration Process - Complete Steps:

Part A: Before Registration - Preparation

Step 1: Determine GST Applicability
- Calculate annual turnover
- Check if interstate supply
- Identify business category
- Check exemption eligibility

Step 2: Gather Required Documents

For Proprietorship:
- PAN card of proprietor (mandatory)
- Aadhaar card of proprietor
- Business address proof
- Bank account statement/cancelled cheque
- Passport size photograph
- Email ID and mobile number

For Partnership Firm:
- PAN of partnership firm
- PAN of all partners
- Partnership deed
- Address proof of business
- Bank account details
- Photographs of partners
- Authorization letter (if representative)

For Private Limited/LLP:
- PAN of company
- Certificate of incorporation
- Memorandum of Association (MOA)
- Articles of Association (AOA)
- Board resolution authorizing signatory
- Address proof of business
- PAN and Aadhaar of authorized signatory
- Bank account details

Address Proof (any one):
- Electricity bill (not older than 2 months)
- Property tax receipt
- Rent/lease agreement + NOC from owner
- Municipal khata copy

Bank Proof:
- Cancelled cheque with name printed
- Bank statement (front page)
- Bank passbook copy

Part B: Online Registration Process

Step 1: Visit GST Portal
- Go to www.gst.gov.in
- Click on "Services" > "Registration" > "New Registration"
- Or direct link: https://reg.gst.gov.in/registration/

Step 2: Fill Part-A of GST REG-01
Enter:
- Legal name of business
- PAN of business
- Email address (OTP verification)
- Mobile number (OTP verification)
- State/UT
- District

Important:
- Use working email and mobile (will receive all updates)
- Cannot be changed easily later
- One PAN = One GST registration per state

Step 3: OTP Verification
- OTP sent to email and mobile
- Enter both OTPs within 5 minutes
- Click "Proceed"

Step 4: TRN (Temporary Reference Number)
- System generates 15-digit TRN
- Save this TRN carefully
- Valid for 15 days to complete Part-B
- TRN sent to email and mobile

Step 5: Complete Part-B (Main Application)
Login using TRN to fill GST REG-01 Part-B:

5a. Business Details:
   - Trade name
   - Constitution of business
   - Date of business commencement
   - Date of liability
   - Reason for registration
   - State of registration

5b. Promoter/Partners Details:
   - Name, father's name
   - PAN, Aadhaar
   - Mobile, email
   - Designation
   - Residential address
   - Upload photo (max 100KB)

5c. Principal Place of Business:
   - Building/flat number
   - Floor number
   - Name of premises
   - Road/street
   - Locality/village
   - District
   - State
   - PIN code
   - Latitude/longitude (optional but recommended)
   - Nature of possession (own/rented/leased)
   - Upload address proof (max 1MB, PDF/JPEG)

5d. Additional Place of Business (if any):
   - Same details as principal place
   - Can add multiple locations

5e. Goods and Services Details:
   - Select HSN codes for goods
   - Select SAC codes for services
   - Can select multiple codes
   - Should cover all business activities

5f. Bank Account Details:
   - Bank name
   - Account number
   - IFSC code
   - Account type (current/savings/cash credit)
   - Upload cancelled cheque/bank statement
   - Can add multiple accounts

5g. State-specific Information (if applicable):
   - Professional tax number
   - Excise registration number
   - VAT/CST registration
   - Service tax number
   - Any other registrations

5h. Authorized Signatory:
   - Name, PAN, mobile, email
   - Designation
   - Upload authorization letter (on letterhead)
   - Upload PAN card and photo

Step 6: Upload All Documents
File formats: PDF, JPEG
Maximum size: 1MB per file
Ensure clarity and readability

Documents checklist:
✓ Photograph of proprietor/partners/directors
✓ PAN card copies
✓ Aadhaar cards
✓ Business address proof
✓ Incorporation certificate (for companies)
✓ Partnership deed (for partnerships)
✓ Bank statement or cancelled cheque
✓ Authorization letter
✓ NOC from owner (if rented premises)
✓ Board resolution (for companies)

Step 7: Verification and Submission
- Verify all entered information
- Use "Save as Draft" to save progress
- Click checkbox for declaration
- E-verify using:
  Option 1: Aadhaar-based OTP
  Option 2: E-Sign (Digital Signature)
- Submit application

Step 8: Application Reference Number (ARN)
- System generates ARN (15-digit)
- ARN sent to registered email and mobile
- Use ARN to track application status
- Acknowledgment in Form GST REG-02

Step 9: Processing by GST Officer
Timeline:
- Officer reviews application: 3-7 working days
- May seek clarification (Form GST REG-03)
- Must respond within 7 days
- May require physical verification

Step 10: GST Certificate Issuance
- If approved: Certificate in Form GST REG-06
- 15-digit GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number)
- Download certificate from portal
- Certificate is valid indefinitely (unless cancelled)

Format of GSTIN:
Example: 27AABCU9603R1ZM
- First 2 digits: State code (27 = Maharashtra)
- Next 10 digits: PAN number
- 13th digit: Entity number (multiple registrations in same state)
- 14th digit: Z (default)
- 15th digit: Check code

Post-Registration Compliance:

1. Display GSTIN:
- On invoices (mandatory)
- At business premises
- On website/e-commerce store
- On business stationery

2. Issue GST-Compliant Invoices:
- Include GSTIN of supplier and recipient
- Invoice number (sequential)
- Date of issue
- Description of goods/services
- HSN/SAC code
- Quantity, rate, value
- Taxable amount
- GST rate and amount (CGST, SGST, IGST)
- Total amount
- Place of supply
- Signature/digital signature

3. Maintain Books of Accounts:
- All invoices (sales and purchase)
- Credit/debit notes
- Delivery challans
- Bank statements
- Stock records
- Must be maintained for 6 years

4. File GST Returns:

Monthly Returns (for regular taxpayers):

a) GSTR-1 (Sales return):
- Due date: 11th of next month
- Details of outward supplies (sales)
- Invoice-wise details for B2B sales
- Consolidated for B2C sales
- Can be filed quarterly for turnover < ₹5 crores (with QRMP scheme)

b) GSTR-3B (Summary return with payment):
- Due date: 20th of next month (22nd/24th for some states)
- Summary of sales and purchases
- ITC claimed
- Tax liability and payment
- Mandatory even if no business

Quarterly Returns (for composition scheme):

c) GSTR-4 (Composition return):
- Due date: 18th of month following quarter-end
- Summary of sales
- Tax payment at fixed rate

Annual Returns:

d) GSTR-9 (Annual return):
- Due date: 31st December of next financial year
- Applicable for turnover > ₹2 crores
- Consolidated information of entire year
- Reconciliation with audited accounts

e) GSTR-9C (Reconciliation statement):
- Applicable for turnover > ₹5 crores
- Certified by CA/CMA
- Reconciliation between GSTR-9 and audited financials

5. Pay GST:
- Self-assessment
- Payment through GST portal
- Using NEFT/RTGS/credit/debit card/net banking
- Generate challan (PMT-06)
- Three ledgers: Cash, Credit, Liability

GST Composition Scheme:

What is it?
- Simplified scheme for small taxpayers
- Pay tax at fixed rate of turnover
- Minimal compliance
- Cannot collect GST from customers

Eligibility:
- Turnover up to ₹1.5 crores
- Only intra-state supplies
- Not applicable for certain businesses (ice cream, pan masala, tobacco, etc.)
- No interstate supplies allowed
- No e-commerce sales allowed
- Cannot issue tax invoices (only Bill of Supply)

Tax Rates under Composition:
- Manufacturers: 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST)
- Restaurants: 5% (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST)
- Traders: 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST)
- Service providers: 6% (3% CGST + 3% SGST)

Advantages:
✓ Lower tax rate
✓ Quarterly return (instead of monthly)
✓ Reduced compliance burden
✓ Simple accounting
✓ No tax collection from customers

Disadvantages:
✗ Cannot claim input tax credit
✗ Cannot make interstate sales
✗ Cannot sell on e-commerce platforms
✗ Cannot issue tax invoices
✗ Lower business credibility

How to Opt:
- Select composition scheme while registration, OR
- Opt-in at start of financial year (by 31st March)
- File intimation in Form GST CMP-02
- Cannot exit mid-year (except special circumstances)

Input Tax Credit (ITC):

What is ITC?
- Credit of GST paid on purchases (inputs)
- Can be used to offset GST liability on sales (outputs)
- Reduces cascading effect of taxes
- Available only to regular GST taxpayers (not composition)

Conditions for ITC:
✓ Possession of valid tax invoice
✓ Goods/services received
✓ Tax has been paid to government by supplier
✓ Returns have been filed
✓ Goods/services used for business purposes

Ineligible ITC:
✗ Motor vehicles (except for specific business use)
✗ Food, beverages (except for outward taxable supply)
✗ Membership of clubs, health centers
✗ Travel benefits to employees (personal)
✗ Works contract for personal residence
✗ Goods/services for personal use
✗ GST paid under composition scheme

ITC Matching:
- Your purchases should match supplier's sales return (GSTR-1)
- Auto-populated in GSTR-2A/2B
- Claim only matched ITC
- Reconciliation important

Time Limit:
- ITC can be claimed until:
  a) 30th November of next financial year, OR
  b) Date of filing annual return (GSTR-9)
  Whichever is earlier

Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM):

What is RCM?
- Recipient pays GST instead of supplier
- Applicable in specific scenarios
- Buyer must be GST registered

When RCM Applies:
- Services from unregistered supplier
- Services from advocate, CA, architect (if unregistered)
- Import of services
- GTA services
- Certain specified goods/services

POST-REGISTRATION COMPLIANCE:

E-Invoicing (NOT Part of Registration):

Applicable to:
- Businesses with turnover > ₹5 crores (from April 2023 onwards)
- Mandatory for B2B transactions

E-Invoicing Generation Process (This is DIFFERENT from GST Registration):
1. Generate invoice on accounting software
2. Upload to Invoice Registration Portal (IRP)
3. IRP validates and generates IRN (Invoice Reference Number)
4. IRP generates QR code
5. IRN and QR code on invoice
6. Auto-reported to GST and E-way bill systems

Benefits of E-Invoicing:
✓ Reduced errors
✓ Faster ITC claims
✓ Easy reconciliation
✓ Reduced disputes

E-Way Bill (NOT Part of Registration):

What is E-Way Bill?
- Electronic document for movement of goods
- Required for interstate transport if value > ₹50,000
- Some states require for intrastate movement too

How to Generate:
1. Login to https://ewaybillgst.gov.in
2. Provide transport and goods details
3. Generate 12-digit E-Way Bill Number
4. Valid for specific distance and time
5. Carrier must have EWB copy

Validity:
- Up to 200 km: 1 day
- Every additional 200 km: Additional 1 day
- Can be extended if needed

GST Refund:

Types of Refunds:
1. Excess tax paid
2. Export of goods/services
3. ITC accumulated (inverted duty structure)
4. Tax paid on deemed exports
5. Finalization of provisional assessment

How to Apply:
- File Form GST RFD-01 online
- Attach relevant documents
- Processing time: 60 days
- Refund credited to bank account

GST Penalties and Interest:

Late Fees:
- GSTR-1: ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST)
- GSTR-3B: ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST)
- Maximum: 0.25% of turnover or ₹5,000 per return

Interest on Late Payment:
- 18% per annum
- Calculated from due date till payment date
- On net tax liability

Penalties:
- Tax evasion: 100% of tax amount
- Non-registration despite liability: 10% of tax amount or ₹10,000 (whichever higher)
- Fake invoices: 100% of tax amount or ₹10,000 (whichever higher)
- Wrong ITC claim: 10% of wrongly claimed credit or ₹10,000

GST Amendments/Changes:

Changes Requiring Amendment:
- Change in business name
- Change in business address
- Addition/deletion of partners/directors
- Addition of new place of business
- Change in bank account
- Change in authorized signatory
- Change in contact details

How to Amend:
- Login to GST portal
- Services > Registration > Amendment of Registration Core Fields/Non-Core Fields
- Make changes
- Submit with supporting documents
- Some changes require approval, some are auto-approved

Core Field Changes (need approval):
- Legal name
- Address of principal place of business
- Change in constitution

Non-Core Changes (auto-approved):
- Email, mobile number
- Bank accounts
- Additional places of business
- Goods/services details

GST Cancellation:

Reasons for Cancellation:
- Business discontinued
- Transfer of business
- Change in constitution
- Registered under composition
- Death of proprietor
- Amalgamation/merger/demerger

Voluntary Cancellation:
- Apply in Form GST REG-16
- Submit online with reason
- Officer may verify
- Certificate in Form GST REG-19

Cancellation by Officer:
- If returns not filed for 6 months
- Non-compliance
- Obtained by fraud
- Notice in Form GST REG-17
- Show cause within 7 days
- Order in Form GST REG-19

Post-Cancellation:
- Final return in Form GSTR-10 (within 3 months)
- Pay all outstanding dues
- Can apply for revocation within 30 days

7. Not maintaining proper records
8. Not updating changes in registration
9. Issuing invoices without GSTIN
10. Not paying tax on reverse charge purchases

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: Can I have multiple GSTIN?
A: Yes, you need separate GSTIN for each state where you have business operations.

Q2: Is GST registration free?
A: Yes, there are no government fees for GST registration.

Q3: How long does registration take?
A: Typically 3-7 working days if documents are correct.

Q4: Can I cancel GST registration anytime?
A: Yes, but you must have valid reason and file final return.

Q5: What if my supplier is not registered?
A: You may need to pay GST under reverse charge mechanism.

Q6: Can I revise returns after filing?
A: No, but you can make corrections in next return for certain types of errors.

Q7: Do I need CA for GST compliance?
A: Not mandatory, but recommended for businesses with turnover > ₹5 crores.

Q8: Is Aadhaar mandatory for GST?
A: Not mandatory but helps in quick verification and authentication.

Q9: Can I operate without GST if turnover is low?
A: Yes, if annual turnover is below ₹40 lakhs/₹20 lakhs threshold.

Q10: What is difference between GSTIN and PAN?
A: PAN is permanent tax identifier, GSTIN is GST-specific identifier linked to PAN.

Q11: Can I claim ITC on business meals?
A: No, ITC on food/beverages not allowed except for outward taxable supply of same.

Q12: What happens if I miss return deadline?
A: Late fees of ₹50/day + interest @18% on tax due will be charged.

Q13: Can I change from composition to regular scheme?
A: Yes, but only at start of financial year by filing intimation.

Q14: Is HSN/SAC code mandatory on invoices?
A: Yes, 4-digit for turnover < ₹5 crores, 6-digit for > ₹5 crores.

Q15: Can I get refund of ITC accumulated?
A: Yes, in case of inverted duty structure, exports, or accumulated credit.
