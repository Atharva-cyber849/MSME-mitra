Udyam Registration for MSMEs - Complete Guide

Comprehensive Guide to MSME Registration in India

What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam Registration is the official online registration portal for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in India. Launched on July 1, 2020, replacing the erstwhile UA

M and EM-II registration processes. It is a completely paperless, self-declaration-based process with instant certificate generation.

Administered by: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)
Portal: https://udyamregistration.gov.in
Helpline: 1800-180-6763
Email: champions@gov.in

Key Features:
✓ Completely online and paperless
✓ 100% free of cost
✓ Instant certificate generation
✓ Based on self-declaration
✓ Lifetime validity
✓ Pan-India recognition
✓ Aadhaar-based authentication
✓ Auto-integration with GSTIN and ITR

Classification of MSMEs (Since July 1, 2020):

Under the new definition, MSMEs are classified based on BOTH investment in plant & machinery/equipment AND annual turnover:

1. MICRO ENTERPRISE:
   Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment: Up to ₹1 Crore
   AND
   Annual Turnover: Up to ₹5 Crore
   
   Examples:
   - Small retail shops
   - Home-based manufacturing
   - Local service providers
   - Street vendors with formal setup
   - Small-scale production units

2. SMALL ENTERPRISE:
   Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment: Up to ₹10 Crore
   AND
   Annual Turnover: Up to ₹50 Crore
   
   Examples:
   - Medium-sized manufacturing units
   - Regional distribution businesses
   - Service firms with multiple locations
   - Food processing units
   - Garment manufacturing

3. MEDIUM ENTERPRISE:
   Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment: Up to ₹50 Crore
   AND
   Annual Turnover: Up to ₹250 Crore
   
   Examples:
   - Large manufacturing facilities
   - Multi-state operations
   - Export-oriented units
   - Technology companies
   - Pharmaceutical units

Important Notes on Classification:

a) Composite Criteria:
   - Both investment AND turnover limits must be satisfied
   - If either exceeds the limit, moves to next category
   - Example: Investment ₹8 Cr, Turnover ₹60 Cr = MEDIUM (not Small)

b) Investment Calculation:
   - Plant & Machinery: For manufacturing enterprises
   - Equipment: For service enterprises
   - Includes all tangible and intangible assets
   - Original cost (not depreciated value)
   - Excludes land and building cost
   - GST paid on these assets is excluded

c) Turnover Calculation:
   - Based on Income Tax Return or GST Return
   - Whichever is available
   - Export turnover included
   - All sources of revenue included

d) No Distinction Between Manufacturing and Service:
   - Previous UAM had different criteria for both
   - Now unified classification for all sectors

Complete Udyam Registration Process:

Eligibility Criteria:

✓ Any enterprise engaged in manufacturing/production, processing, or preservation of goods
✓ Any enterprise engaged in providing services
✓ Can be proprietorship, partnership, LLP, company, cooperative, trust
✓ Must have Aadhaar number (for proprietor/partners/directors)
✓ Must be starting a new enterprise OR already running one
✓ Investment and turnover within MSME limits
✓ No restriction on location (anywhere in India)

Who Can Apply:

- Proprietor (using own Aadhaar)
- Partner (any one partner's Aadhaar for partnership/LLP)
- Director (any authorized director's Aadhaar for companies)
- Managing Trustee (for trusts)
- President/Chairman (for cooperative societies)
- Self Help Groups
- Any other legal entities

Documents/Information Required:

Mandatory:
1. Aadhaar number of entrepreneur (must be active)
2. PAN of business (linked to Aadhaar)
3. Business details:
   - Name of enterprise
   - Type of organization
   - PAN of enterprise
   - Address with PIN code
   - Date of commencement
   - Bank account details (account number, IFSC)
   - Major activity/products (NIC 2-digit code)
   - Number of persons employed

Optional (if available):
4. GSTIN (if registered)
5. Import Export Code (IEC)
6. Previous UAM/EM-II number (if migrating)

Step-by-Step Registration Process:

STEP 1: Access the Portal
- Open browser and go to: https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- Click on "For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME or those with EM-Part II"
- Alternatively, for UAM holders: Click on "For those already having Registration as UAM"

STEP 2: Enter Aadhaar and PAN
- Enter the 12-digit Aadhaar number of the proprietor/authorized person
- Enter PAN of the business entity
- For proprietorship: PAN of proprietor
- For partnership/LLP: PAN of firm
- For company: PAN of company
- Check the validation box "I hereby confirm..."
- Click "Validate & Generate OTP"

Important Points:
- Aadhaar must be active and valid
- PAN must be linked to Aadhaar (check at www.incometax.gov.in)
- Mobile number registered with Aadhaar will receive OTP
- If Aadhaar-PAN not linked, registration will fail

STEP 3: OTP Verification
- OTP sent to mobile number registered with Aadhaar
- Enter 6-digit OTP within 5 minutes
- If not received, click "Resend OTP"
- Maximum 3 OTP attempts
- After verification, registration form opens

STEP 4: Fill Registration Form

The form has 9 sections to fill:

Section 1: Entrepreneur Details (Auto-filled from Aadhaar)
- Name of entrepreneur
- Gender
- Date of birth
- Category (General/SC/ST/OBC)
- Physically handicapped (Yes/No)
- Mobile number
- Email address (enter manually)

Tips:
- Verify auto-filled name matches Aadhaar
- Provide active email (certificate will be sent here)
- Select correct category (important for scheme benefits)

Section 2: Type of Organisation
Select one:
- Proprietorship
- Partnership
- Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)
- Company
- Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- Cooperative Society
- Society/Trust
- Self Help Group

Based on selection, additional fields appear

Section 3: PAN and Name of Enterprise
- PAN of enterprise (pre-filled)
- Official name of enterprise
  * For propriership: Can be proprietor's name or trade name
  * For company/LLP: Exact registered name
  * For partnership: Registered firm name
- Name in local language (optional)

Section 4: Address Details

Official Address of Enterprise:
- Flat/Door/Block number
- Name of Premises/Building
- Village/Town
- Block
- Road/Street/Lane
- City
- District (auto-filled based on PIN)
- State (auto-filled based on PIN)
- PIN Code (6-digit)
- Mobile number of enterprise
- Email of enterprise
- Whether premises is (select): Owned/Rented/Leased/Shared

Additional Location:
- Can add multiple locations (branches/units)
- Same details as above for each location

Section 5: Bank Details
- Bank account number
- Bank name
- Branch name
- IFSC code
- Account type (Current/Savings/Cash Credit/Overdraft)

Tips:
- Use business bank account (preferably current account)
- For proprietorship, savings account also acceptable
- Verify IFSC code (11 characters)

Section 6: Major Activity/Product
- Select NIC (National Industrial Classification) 2-digit code
- Example:
  * 10: Manufacture of food products
  * 13: Manufacture of textiles
  * 14: Manufacture of wearing apparel
  * 15: Manufacture of leather products
  * 47: Retail trade
  * 56: Food and beverage service activities
  * 62: Computer programming services
  * 95: Repair of computers and personal goods
- Choose the code that best describes your main activity
- Can select only ONE code

Section 7: Details of Employment Generated
Enter number of persons employed:
- Male
- Female
- Other gender
- Total (auto-calculated)

Include:
- Owner/partners/directors
- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees (count as per actual engagement)
- Contract workers (if on regular basis)

Section 8: Other Details

a) Date of Commencement/Incorporation:
   - Date when business started operations
   - For existing businesses: Actual start date
   - For new businesses: Expected start date (can be future date)
   - Format: DD/MM/YYYY

b) Previous Registration Numbers (if applicable):
   - DIC (District Industries Centre) number
   - UAM (Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum) number
   - EM Part-II number
   - Any other previous MSME registration

c) GSTIN (if registered for GST):
   - Enter 15-digit GSTIN
   - If not registered, leave blank
   - System auto-fetches turnover from GSTN database

d) Import Export Code (IEC) (if applicable):
   - 10-digit IEC from DGFT
   - If not involved in import/export, leave blank

e) Investment and Turnover:
   
   For NEW enterprises (less than 1 year old):
   - Investment in Plant & Machinery or Equipment: Enter amount in ₹
   - Turnover: Leave as 0 (not applicable for new units)
   
   For EXISTING enterprises:
   - Investment in Plant & Machinery or Equipment: Enter amount in ₹
   - Turnover: Will be AUTO-FETCHED from GST return or ITR
     * If GSTIN provided: From GSTR-3B or GSTR-1
     * If no GSTIN but ITR filed: From Income Tax Return
     * If both not available: Self-declaration accepted

f) Type of Enterprise:
   - Auto-classified based on investment and turnover
   - Micro/Small/Medium displayed automatically
   - Cannot be manually changed

Section 9: Declaration
- Read the declaration statement
- Check the box: "I hereby declare that..."
- Confirms all information is true
- Penalty for false declaration

STEP 5: Final Submission
- Review all entered information
- Click "Submit & Get Final OTP"
- OTP sent again to Aadhaar-linked mobile
- Enter OTP
- Click "Submit"

STEP 6: Certificate Generation
- After submission, Udyam Registration Number generated instantly
- Format: UDYAM-XX-00-0000000
  * XX: State code
  * 00: District code
  * 0000000: Unique 7-digit number
- Certificate displayed on screen in PDF format
- Also sent to registered email
- Contains:
  * Udyam Registration Number
  * Enterprise name and address
  * Type of enterprise (Micro/Small/Medium)
  * Date of commencement
  * Date of udyam registration
  * Major activity
  * QR code for verification

STEP 7: Download and Save
- Download PDF certificate
- Take printout
- Save digital copy safely
- Display at business premises (recommended)

Post-Registration Actions:

Updating Udyam Registration:

When to Update:
- Change in investment or turnover
- Change in address
- Change in bank account
- Addition of new activity
- Change in organization type
- Any other detail change

How to Update:

Step 1: Visit portal and login
- Go to https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- Click "Update Details (UAM/Udyam Registration)"
- Enter Udyam Registration Number OR Aadhaar
- Get OTP and verify

Step 2: Modify Details
- Edit required fields
- Cannot change: PAN, Aadhaar, Entrepreneur name
- Can change: Address, bank, investment, turnover, activity, etc.

Step 3: Submit
- Submit with OTP verification
- Updated certificate generated immediately
- New certificate sent to email

Note: Updates are free and can be done unlimited times

Common Issues and Troubleshooting:

Issue 1: Aadhaar-PAN Not Linked
Solution:
- Visit www.incometax.gov.in
- Go to "Link Aadhaar" service
- Enter PAN, Aadhaar, and name
- Verify and submit
- Wait 24-48 hours
- Try Udyam registration again

Issue 2: OTP Not Received
Solution:
- Check if mobile number is updated in Aadhaar
- Visit nearby Aadhaar center to update
- Ensure mobile number is active
- Check SMS inbox and spam folder
- Try resend OTP
- Wait for at least 2 minutes before resending

Issue 3: Wrong Details Auto-Filled
Solution:
- Check source data (Aadhaar/PAN/GST/ITR)
- Update at source first
- Wait for system sync (24-48 hours)
- Try registration again
- Contact helpdesk if persists

Issue 4: Unable to Access Certificate After Registration
Solution:
- Go to "Print/Verify" section
- Enter Udyam Number or Aadhaar
- Verify with OTP
- Download certificate again
- Check registered email (including spam)

Issue 5: Classification Shows Wrong Category
Solution:
- System auto-calculates based on investment + turnover
- Check if both criteria are correctly entered
- Investment and turnover together determine category
- Update if initial figures were wrong
- Cannot manually override classification

Issue 6: Previous UAM Not Getting Migrated
Solution:
- Use separate option for UAM holders
- Enter UAM number correctly (12-character format)
- Ensure Aadhaar linked to UAM is used
- Contact previous DIC office if issues persist
- Can register fresh if migration fails

FAQs - Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: Is Udyam Registration mandatory?
A: Not mandatory by law, but essential to avail MSME benefits, government schemes, priority lending, and government procurement opportunities.

Q2: What is the fee for Udyam Registration?
A: Completely FREE. No government fee at any stage. Beware of agents charging money.

Q3: How long does it take to get the certificate?
A: Instant! Certificate is generated immediately after form submission and OTP verification.

Q4: Can I register multiple businesses?
A: Yes, but each business needs separate Udyam registration with unique PAN.

Q5: What if I don't have GST registration?
A: Not required. Udyam can be done without GST. But turnover will be from ITR or self-declared.

Q6: Can I update details later?
A: Yes, unlimited times, free of cost. Simply login with Aadhaar and update.

Q7: Is physical verification required?
A: No physical verification or document submission required. Completely online based on self-declaration.

Q8: What if my business grows and exceeds MSME limits?
A: Update your registration with new investment/turnover. System will auto-classify. Benefits may change based on new category.

Q9: Can partnership firm have multiple Udyam registrations?
A: One PAN = One Udyam. But if multiple units with different PANs, then yes.

Q10: Do I need to renew Udyam registration?
A: No renewal required. Lifetime validity. Just update if details change.

Q11: Can I get loan without Udyam?
A: Yes, but won't get priority sector benefits, lower interest, or collateral-free loans under CGTMSE.

Q12: What if I made mistake during registration?
A: Simply login and update the incorrect information. Certificate will be regenerated.

Q13: Is Udyam valid for government tenders?
A: Yes, essential for participating in government procurement. Provides EMD exemption and preference.

Q14: Can I register before starting business?
A: Yes, you can provide future commencement date. Update turnover later.

Q15: What documents are needed to show investment?
A: No documents needed during registration. But keep purchase invoices ready in case of future audit or verification.

Q16: Can unregistered partnership get Udyam?
A: Yes, even unregistered partnerships can get Udyam. But must have PAN of firm.

Q17: How to check if Udyam certificate is genuine?
A: Visit udyamregistration.gov.in > Print/Verify section > Enter Udyam Number > System will show if valid.

Q18: Can trust or NGO get Udyam?
A: Yes, if engaged in manufacturing/service activities. Select appropriate organization type.

Q19: What if turnover shown is wrong (auto-fetched)?
A: Contact GST helpdesk or income tax to correct source data. Then update Udyam.

Q20: Is Aadhaar-based e-KYC mandatory?
A: Yes, Aadhaar is must for registration. Provides authentication and prevents duplication.
