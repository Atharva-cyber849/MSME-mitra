MUDRA Loans - Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

Complete Guide to MUDRA Loans for Micro Enterprises

What is MUDRA?
Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency (MUDRA) was launched on April 8, 2015, to provide funding to micro enterprises. The Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) provides loans up to ₹10 lakhs to micro enterprises without collateral.

Objective:
- Regulate and refinance microfinance institutions
- Fund micro-enterprises engaged in income-generating activities
- Promote entrepreneurship at grassroots level
- Financial inclusion of small businesses

Three Categories of MUDRA Loans:

1. SHISHU (Baby Step)
Loan Amount: Up to ₹50,000
Target: Startups and new businesses
Purpose:
- Starting a new business
- Working capital for small shops
- Street vendors
- Service providers
- Small manufacturing units
- Agricultural allied activities

Features:
- No collateral required
- Simple documentation
- Quick processing (7-15 days)
- Interest rates: 8-12% per annum
- Repayment: 1-5 years
- MUDRA card provided (like credit card)

Examples:
- Tea stall/small eatery
- Vegetable/fruit vendors
- Small repair shops
- Beauty parlor (basic)
- Tailoring/embroidery
- Papad/pickle making
- Photography (small scale)

2. KISHORE (Adolescent Stage)
Loan Amount: ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakhs
Target: Established businesses looking to expand
Purpose:
- Business expansion
- Purchase of equipment/machinery
- Working capital enhancement
- Inventory building
- Technology upgradation

Features:
- Minimal collateral (for amounts above ₹2 lakhs in some cases)
- Standard documentation
- Processing time: 15-25 days
- Interest rates: 9-13% per annum
- Repayment: 3-7 years
- Can be used for multiple purposes

Examples:
- Small retail shops
- Food processing units
- Garment manufacturing
- Transport operators (2-3 vehicles)
- Beauty salon (with equipment)
- Small printing press
- Catering services
- Computer training center

3. TARUN (Young Stage)
Loan Amount: ₹5 lakhs to ₹10 lakhs
Target: Well-established businesses for expansion/modernization
Purpose:
- Major business expansion
- New machinery/technology
- Infrastructure development
- Working capital for larger operations
- Market development

Features:
- Detailed business plan required
- Collateral may be required (varies by bank)
- Processing time: 20-40 days
- Interest rates: 10-14% per annum
- Repayment: 5-7 years
- Larger ticket size for growth

Examples:
- Manufacturing units
- Service sector businesses
- Trading establishments
- Transport business (4+ vehicles)
- Modern food processing
- Hotel/restaurant
- Diagnostic centers
- Large retail outlets

Complete Eligibility Criteria:

General Eligibility:
✓ Indian citizen/resident
✓ Age: 18 years and above (up to 65 years for some banks)
✓ Business should be operational or viable plan for new business
✓ Manufacturing, trading, or service sector
✓ No existing loan defaults or bad credit history
✓ Should not be defaulter in any bank/financial institution
✓ Business should generate income

Sector-wise Eligibility:

Manufacturing:
- Small-scale manufacturing
- Food processing
- Textile/garment production
- Paper products
- Handicrafts
- Agriculture equipment repair

Trading:
- Retail shops
- Wholesale traders
- Commission agents
- Hawkers/street vendors
- Small stores

Services:
- Transport services
- Beauty/wellness services
- Healthcare services
- Education/training
- Repair & maintenance
- Professional services
- Food services

Excluded Activities:
- Agricultural activities (crop production, animal husbandry)
- Speculative business
- Illegal activities
- Activities not generating income

Documents Required:

Mandatory Documents:
1. Identity Proof:
   - Aadhaar card (mandatory)
   - PAN card (mandatory)
   - Voter ID/Passport/Driving License

2. Address Proof:
   - Aadhaar card
   - Utility bills (electricity, water, gas - not older than 2 months)
   - Rent agreement (if rented premises)
   - Property papers (if owned)

3. Business Proof:
   - Shop/establishment registration
   - Trade license
   - GST registration (if applicable)
   - Udyam registration (recommended)
   - Business address proof

4. Financial Documents:
   - Last 6 months bank statements
   - Income tax returns (last 2 years if applicable)
   - Sales/purchase invoices
   - Balance sheet and P&L (for existing businesses)

5. Other Documents:
   - Passport size photographs (2-3)
   - Cancelled cheque
   - Business quotations (for equipment/machinery)
   - Project report (for Tarun category)
   - Caste certificate (if applicable for reserved category benefits)

Application Process - Step by Step:

Step 1: Choose Lender
Options:
- Public sector banks (SBI, PNB, BOB, etc.)
- Private sector banks (ICICI, HDFC, Axis, etc.)
- Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)
- Small Finance Banks
- MicroFinance Institutions (MFIs)
- NBFCs (Non-Banking Financial Companies)

Step 2: Visit Udyami Mitra Portal (Optional)
- Website: www.udyamimitra.in
- Online application
- Track application status
- Get nearest lender details

Step 3: Visit Bank/Lender
- Meet loan officer
- Discuss business plan
- Understand terms and conditions
- Get application form

Step 4: Fill Application Form
Provide details:
- Personal information
- Business details
- Loan amount required
- Purpose of loan
- Repayment capacity
- Existing liabilities (if any)

Step 5: Submit Documents
- Submit all required documents
- Bank will verify authenticity
- May conduct field visit
- Interview with loan officer

Step 6: Loan Processing
- Credit appraisal
- Business viability check
- CIBIL score verification
- Technical feasibility (if applicable)
- Decision: 7-30 days depending on amount

Step 7: Loan Sanction
- Sanction letter issued
- Terms and conditions specified
- Interest rate finalized
- Repayment schedule provided

Step 8: Loan Disbursement
- Sign loan agreement
- Execute necessary documents
- Loan amount credited to account
- MUDRA card issued (for Shishu)
- Can be disbursed in stages (for Tarun)

Public Sector Banks:
- Shishu: 7.5-10%
- Kishore: 8.5-11.5%
- Tarun: 9.5-12.5%

Private Sector Banks:
- Shishu: 8-11%
- Kishore: 9-12%
- Tarun: 10-13%

MicroFinance Institutions:
- Shishu: 10-14%
- Kishore: 11-15%
- Tarun: 12-16%

Note: Rates vary by bank, location, borrower profile, and market conditions

Repayment Terms:

Shishu:
- Tenure: 1-5 years
- EMI or bullet payment
- Weekly/monthly installments
- Flexible repayment options

Kishore:
- Tenure: 3-7 years
- Monthly EMI (Equal Monthly Installments)
- Moratorium: 3-6 months (if applicable)

Tarun:
- Tenure: 5-7 years
- Monthly EMI
- Moratorium: 6-12 months (for manufacturing)
- Step-up/step-down EMI options

Prepayment:
- Allowed in most cases
- No prepayment penalty (usually)
- Helps reduce interest burden

MUDRA Card:

What is MUDRA Card?
- Like a debit card/credit card
- Provided for Shishu category
- Can withdraw as per requirement
- Interest only on amount used
- Revolving credit facility

Features:
- Valid for loan tenure
- Use through ATM or POS
- No need to visit branch repeatedly
- Flexible fund access
- Monitor through mobile banking

Common Reasons for Rejection:

1. Poor Credit History:
- Existing loan defaults
- Low CIBIL score (below 650)
- Bounced cheques
- Previous loan write-offs

2. Inadequate Documentation:
- Incomplete application
- Invalid/expired documents
- Mismatch in information

3. Unviable Business:
- Unclear business model
- No market potential
- High risk sector
- Unrealistic projections

4. Insufficient Repayment Capacity:
- Low income
- High existing liabilities
- No collateral for higher amounts

5. Other Reasons:
- Age not meeting criteria
- Business in excluded category
- Applicant is willful defaulter
- Suspicious activities

Tips for Loan Approval:

1. Maintain Good Credit Score:

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: Can I apply for MUDRA loan if I have existing loans?
A: Yes, provided you have good repayment track record and capacity for additional EMI.

Q2: Is Udyam registration mandatory for MUDRA loan?
A: Not mandatory but highly recommended as it provides additional benefits.

Q3: Can I get MUDRA loan online?
A: Application can be initiated online through Udyami Mitra portal, but verification happens at branch.

Q4: What is the processing time?
A: Typically 7-30 days depending on loan amount and bank.

Q5: Are there any processing fees?
A: Usually minimal or no processing fee. Check with your bank.

Q6: Can I increase loan amount later?
A: Yes, you can apply for higher category after repaying existing loan or based on business growth.

Q7: Is insurance required?
A: Not mandatory but recommended. Some banks may require insurance for higher amounts.

Q8: Can NRIs apply?
A: No, MUDRA loans are for Indian residents only.

Q9: What if I face difficulty in repayment?
A: Contact bank immediately. Banks may restructure loan or provide moratorium.

Q10: Can I get MUDRA loan for multiple businesses?
A: Yes, if you have capacity to repay and businesses are viable.
