1. PM VISHWAKARMA SCHEME
===============================================

Launch Date: September 2023
Ministry: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises

Overview:
PM Vishwakarma is a comprehensive package for traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in 18 traditional trades. The scheme provides end-to-end support including skill training, modern tools, credit support, and market linkages.

Target Beneficiaries:
Traditional artisans and craftspeople working with hands and tools in the following 18 family-based traditional trades:
1. Carpenter (Badhai)
2. Boat Maker
3. Armorer
4. Blacksmith (Lohar)
5. Hammer and Toolkit Maker
6. Locksmith
7. Goldsmith
8. Potter (Kumhar)
9. Sculptor/Stone Carver (Moorti Kaar/Stone Mason)
10. Cobbler/Shoe Maker (Mochi)
11. Mason (Raj Mistri)
12. Basket/Mat/Broom Maker/Coir Weaver
13. Traditional Doll and Toy Maker
14. Barber (Nai)
15. Garland Maker
16. Washerman (Dhobi)
17. Tailor (Darzi)
18. Fishing Net Maker

Eligibility Criteria:
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Engaged in eligible trade on self-employment/business basis
- Should not have availed loan under similar credit-based schemes in past 5 years
- Registration with PM Vishwakarma portal mandatory
- Family member (husband, wife, unmarried children) should not have availed benefits

Financial Benefits:

1. Stipend During Training:
   - ₹500 per day during basic training
   - ₹500 per day during advanced training

2. Toolkit Incentive:
   - ₹15,000 one-time grant for purchasing modern tools
   - Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank account

3. Credit Support:
   Tier 1: Initial loan up to ₹1 lakh
   - Interest rate: 5% (concessional)
   - Repayment: 18 months
   - No collateral required
   - Credit Guarantee coverage

   Tier 2: Additional loan up to ₹2 lakhs
   - Available after successful repayment of Tier 1
   - Interest rate: 5%
   - Repayment: 30 months
   - Interest subvention provided by government

4. Digital Marketing Support:
   - E-commerce platform onboarding
   - National Marketing Committee support
   - Trade fairs and exhibitions
   - Platform fees support

Non-Financial Benefits:

1. Skill Training:
   Basic Training: 40 hours (5-7 days)
   - Modern techniques and tools
   - Business development
   - Quality improvement
   
   Advanced Training: 120 hours (15 days)
   - Skill upgradation
   - Design improvement
   - New technology adoption

2. Certificate of Recognition:
   - PM Vishwakarma certificate
   - Identity card with QR code
   - Digital profile on portal

3. Market Linkages:
   - Government e-Marketplace (GeM) access
   - E-commerce platform integration
   - Quality certification support
   - Branding and packaging assistance

Registration Process:

Step 1: Mobile Verification
- Visit pmvishwakarma.gov.in
- Register mobile number
- Verify through OTP
- Complete Aadhaar-based e-KYC

Step 2: Application Submission
- Select family-based trade
- Fill personal details
- Upload required documents
- Submit application online

Step 3: Verification
- Common Service Center (CSC) verification
- Biometric authentication
- Document verification
- Trade verification

Step 4: Certificate Issuance
- PM Vishwakarma certificate
- Unique ID generation
- Enrollment in training program

Step 5: Training and Benefits
- Attend basic training
- Receive toolkit incentive
- Apply for credit support
- Access market linkages

Documents Required:
- Aadhaar card (mandatory)
- Bank account details
- Mobile number (registered with Aadhaar)
- Photograph
- Trade-related document (if available)

Implementation:
- Nodal Ministry: Ministry of MSME
- Implementing agencies: CSCs, DIC, KVIC
- Training partners: Approved institutes
- Credit partners: Public sector banks, RRBs

Budget Allocation: ₹13,000 Crores for 5 years

5. TECHNOLOGY UPGRADATION SCHEMES
===============================================

A. Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (CLCSS)

Objective: Facilitate technology upgradation in MSMEs

Subsidy:
- 15% of purchase price of plant & machinery
- Maximum subsidy: ₹15 lakhs

Eligibility:
- Registered MSMEs
- Investment in new machinery
- Approved list of technologies

Process:
1. Purchase approved machinery
2. Apply through bank
3. Install and commission
4. Submit claim to bank
5. Bank forwards to CLCSS office
6. Subsidy credited to loan account

Technologies Covered:
- Manufacturing: 51 sub-sectors
- Services: IT, logistics, cold chain
- Quality certification systems
- Energy-efficient technologies

B. Technology and Quality Upgradation (TQU) Support

Components:
1. Technology acquisition support
2. Quality certification reimbursement:
   - ISO 9000/14001: 75% reimbursement
   - HACCP: 75% reimbursement
   - ZED: 80% reimbursement

3. Patent support:
   - Domestic patent: 50% reimbursement
   - International patent: 50% up to ₹5 lakhs

4. Testing and R&D support

C. Lean Manufacturing Competitiveness Scheme

Objective: Improve manufacturing productivity

Support:
- Diagnostic study: 80-90% subsidy
- Handholding: 60-80% subsidy
- Implementation support

Benefits:
- Productivity improvement: 25-30%
- Waste reduction: 20-25%
- Quality improvement: 15-20%

7. ZED CERTIFICATION SCHEME
===============================================

Full Form: Zero Defect Zero Effect

Objective:
Promote quality consciousness and environmentally sustainable manufacturing

Certification Levels:
- Bronze
- Silver
- Gold
- Diamond

Subsidy Structure:

Micro Enterprises:
- Consultancy: 80% subsidy
- Certification: 80% subsidy
- Maximum: ₹3 lakhs

Small Enterprises:
- Consultancy: 60% subsidy
- Certification: 60% subsidy
- Maximum: ₹5 lakhs

Medium Enterprises:
- Consultancy: 50% subsidy
- Certification: 50% subsidy
- Maximum: ₹7 lakhs

Benefits:
- Quality improvement
- Environmental sustainability
- Market credibility
- Government procurement preference
- Export competitiveness

Implementation Process:
1. Self-assessment through ZED portal
2. Select ZED consultant and certification body
3. Gap analysis and documentation
4. Implementation of improvements
5. Assessment and rating
6. Certificate issuance
7. Subsidy claim

Website: zed.msme.gov.in

8. MSME SAMADHAAN PORTAL
===============================================

Purpose: Resolution of delayed payment issues

Legal Backing: MSMED Act, 2006

Features:

1. Online Filing of Delayed Payment Cases
- Simple online application
- Upload supporting documents
- Track case status

2. Automatic Notices
- Notice to buyer
- Email and SMS alerts

3. Conciliation and Arbitration
- Through Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Councils (MSEFCs)
- State-level councils
- Fast-track resolution

4. Compound Interest Calculation
- Automatic calculation of interest
- Three times of bank rate
- From due date to payment date

How to File:

Step 1: Registration
- Visit samadhaan.msme.gov.in
- Register with Udyam number
- Verify email and mobile

Step 2: File Application
- Enter buyer details
- Provide invoice/order information
- Specify amount and due date
- Upload supporting documents

Step 3: MSEFC Action
- Case assigned to council
- Conciliation attempt
- Arbitration if needed
- Award passed

Step 4: Recovery
- Award enforceable as court decree
- Recovery through buyer's bank
- Penalty on buyer for default

Success Rate: 60-70% resolution within 90 days

12. HOW TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT SCHEME
===============================================

Decision Framework:

For New Business (No existing unit):
- PMEGP: If you need subsidy and credit
- Stand-Up India: If SC/ST/Woman with bigger project
- Mudra-Shishu: Very small loan requirement
- PM Vishwakarma: If traditional artisan

For Existing Business:
- Mudra loans: For expansion up to ₹10 lakhs
- CGTMSE: For collateral-free loans
- CLCSS: For technology upgradation
- ZED: For quality certification

For Specific Needs:
- Working capital: Mudra, Bank loans with CGTMSE
- Machinery: CLCSS, regular loans
- Quality: ZED certification
- Marketing: MDA schemes
- Training: PM Vishwakarma, CDP
- Delayed payments: MSME Samadhaan

By Category:
- SC/ST: Stand-Up India, PMEGP (higher subsidy)
- Women: Stand-Up India, PMEGP (higher subsidy)
- Artisans: PM Vishwakarma
- General: PMEGP, Mudra, CGTMSE

13. APPLICATION TIPS AND BEST PRACTICES
===============================================

Before Applying:

1. Research Thoroughly:
   - Read complete guidelines
   - Check eligibility carefully
   - Understand documentation requirements
   - Calculate realistic project cost

2. Choose Right Scheme:
   - Match your profile with scheme
   - Check exclusion list
   - Verify activity coverage

3. Prepare Documents:
   - Keep all documents ready
   - Ensure proper attestation
   - Recent documents (not older than 6 months)
   - Clear scanned copies

During Application:

4. Online Application:
   - Use laptop/desktop (not mobile)
   - Stable internet connection
   - Fill carefully without errors
   - Save application ID

5. Project Report:
   - Realistic and detailed
   - Market analysis included
   - Proper cost estimation
   - Clear employment generation plan

6. Bank Selection:
   - Choose MSME-friendly bank
   - Local branch preferred
   - Check bank's track record

After Application:

7. Follow-up:
   - Track application regularly
   - Respond to queries promptly
   - Attend interview prepared
   - Keep communication documented

8. Documentation:
   - Keep copies of all submissions
   - Maintain email correspondence
   - Note important dates
   - Record officer names and contacts

Common Mistakes to Avoid:

❌ Incomplete application
❌ Mismatch in documents
❌ Unrealistic project cost
❌ Ignoring exclusion list
❌ Multiple applications for same project
❌ False information
❌ Missing deadlines
❌ Poor project report

15. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
===============================================

General Questions:

Q1: Can I apply for multiple schemes simultaneously?
A: Generally no. Most schemes prohibit availing benefits under multiple similar schemes. However, you can combine different types like PMEGP (credit) + ZED (certification) + MDA (marketing).

Q2: Are these schemes available in all states?
A: Yes, these are central government schemes available pan-India. However, implementation mechanisms may vary by state.

Q3: Is Udyam registration mandatory for all schemes?
A: For most schemes yes, but PM Vishwakarma and PMEGP can be starting point before Udyam registration.

Q4: How long does approval take?
A: Varies by scheme:
- PM Vishwakarma: 15-30 days
- PMEGP: 60-90 days
- Stand-Up India: 30-45 days
- CGTMSE: With loan sanction
- CLCSS: 90-120 days

Q5: Can I apply online for all schemes?
A: Yes, all major schemes have online application portals.

Eligibility Questions:

Q6: I defaulted on a loan 10 years ago. Am I eligible?
A: Most schemes check CIBIL score. If default is settled and sufficient time has passed, you may be eligible. Check specific scheme guidelines.

Q7: Can NRIs apply for these schemes?
A: Generally, schemes are for Indian residents only. NRIs can invest but not avail subsidies.

Q8: Is there an upper age limit?
A: Most schemes have no upper age limit. Minimum is usually 18 years.

Q9: Can government employees apply?
A: No, most schemes exclude government employees. Retired employees can apply.

Q10: Can companies or only individuals apply?
A: Varies by scheme:
- Individual: PM Vishwakarma, PMEGP, Stand-Up India
- Both: Mudra, CGTMSE, CLCSS
- Companies/LLPs: ZED, MDA

Financial Questions:

Q11: Is the subsidy taxable?
A: Generally, subsidies under PMEGP and similar schemes are not taxable as income. Consult CA for your specific case.

Q12: What if I can't repay the loan?
A: Non-payment affects CIBIL score and future loan eligibility. Communicate with bank for restructuring options. CGTMSE may not cover willful defaults.

Q13: Can I prepay the loan without penalty?
A: Most MSME loans allow prepayment. Check loan agreement terms.

Q14: How is subsidy disbursed?
A: Usually credited to beneficiary account or adjusted against loan.

Q15: What interest rate applies?
A: Varies by scheme and bank:
- PM Vishwakarma: 5%
- PMEGP: 8-12% (market rates)
- Stand-Up India: Bank's MSME rates
- Mudra: 8-12%

Application Process:

Q16: Can I apply offline?
A: Most schemes require online application, but offline support available at DIC, KVIC offices, CSCs.

Q17: Is there an application fee?
A: No fees for government schemes. Beware of agents charging fees.

Q18: Can I edit application after submission?
A: Usually not. Contact concerned office for corrections.

Q19: How to check application status?
A: Login to respective portal or contact implementing agency.

Q20: What if my application is rejected?
A: Check rejection reason, rectify issues, and reapply in next cycle.

Implementation Questions:

Q21: Can I change my business location after approval?
A: Major changes not allowed without permission. Contact implementing agency.

Q22: Can I modify the project after sanction?
A: Minor modifications possible with approval. Major changes may require fresh application.

Q23: When should I start the business?
A: After loan disbursement. Do not start before formalities are complete.

Q24: How to claim subsidy for CLCSS?
A: Through financing bank after machinery installation and commissioning.

Q25: Can I avail CGTMSE for existing loan?
A: No, CGTMSE is for new loans. However, existing loans can be migrated in some cases.
