PMEGP - Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme

Complete Guide to PMEGP Scheme for New Entrepreneurs

What is PMEGP?
PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme) is a credit-linked subsidy scheme launched by the Ministry of MSME. It aims to generate employment opportunities through establishment of micro enterprises in rural and urban areas.

Nodal Agency: Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC)

Scheme Objectives:
- Generate employment opportunities in rural and urban areas
- Bring together widely dispersed traditional artisans and unemployed youth
- Provide continuous and sustainable employment
- Increase wage earning capacity of artisans
- Contribute to rural industrialization

Financial Assistance:

Maximum Project Cost:
- Manufacturing Sector: ₹50 lakhs
- Service/Business Sector: ₹20 lakhs

Subsidy Rates:
1. General Category:
   - Urban Areas: 15% of project cost
   - Rural Areas: 25% of project cost

2. Special Category (SC/ST/OBC/Minorities/Women/Ex-servicemen/Physically Handicapped/NER/Hill and Border areas):
   - Urban Areas: 25% of project cost
   - Rural Areas: 35% of project cost

Beneficiary Contribution:
- General Category: 10% of project cost
- Special Category: 5% of project cost
- Remaining amount: Bank loan

Eligibility Criteria:

Age: Above 18 years

Educational Qualification:
- Minimum 8th pass for projects above ₹10 lakhs in manufacturing
- Minimum 8th pass for projects above ₹5 lakhs in service/business
- Only for self-employment training: No minimum education

Income Criteria:
- Family income should not exceed ₹3 lakhs per annum for service sector
- No income ceiling for manufacturing sector in certain cases

Other Conditions:
- Should not have defaulted on any bank loan
- Assistance under PMEGP only once
- Existing units not eligible (units registered under any other scheme)
- Self-Help Groups (with BPL persons) are eligible
- Institutions registered under Societies Registration Act
- Production Co-operative Societies
- Charitable Trusts

Application Process:

Step 1: Online Registration
- Visit: www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal
- Click on "Application Form for Individual"
- Register with details (name, email, mobile)
- Receive login credentials

Step 2: Fill Application Form
- Login to portal
- Select "Fill your Application Form"
- Provide complete details:
  * Personal information
  * Educational qualification
  * Training details (if any)
  * Project details
  * Bank details
  * Financial information
  * Employment generation plan

Step 3: Document Upload
Required Documents:
- Photograph (passport size)
- Signature
- Educational certificates
- Caste certificate (if applicable)
- Residence proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Ration Card)
- Bank account details
- Project report
- Land/shed documents (if applicable)
- Quotations for machinery/equipment

Step 4: Submission
- Review all details
- Submit application
- Note Application ID for tracking

Step 5: Scrutiny and Interview
- Application forwarded to District KVIC/DIC office
- Scrutiny of documents
- Personal interview/interaction
- Field visit if required
- Technical evaluation

Step 6: Bank Financing
- Eligible applications forwarded to banks
- Bank appraisal
- Loan sanction
- Disbursement of loan and subsidy

Loan Disbursement:

Phase 1 (Before Production):
- 90% of working capital
- 75% of machinery cost
- Subsidy amount

Phase 2 (After Starting Production):
- Remaining 10% working capital
- Remaining 25% machinery cost

Subsidy Release:
- Released in two installments
- First: 50% after investment of beneficiary contribution
- Second: 50% after loan utilization

Margin Money Subsidy:
Directly credited to:
1. Beneficiary's bank account (for working capital)
2. Supplier's account (for fixed assets)

Repayment Terms:

Manufacturing Units:
- Moratorium: 6-12 months
- Repayment Period: 3-7 years

Service/Business Units:
- Moratorium: 3-6 months
- Repayment Period: 3-5 years

Interest Rates:
- As per bank's MSME lending rates
- Usually 8-12% per annum
- Special rates for women/SC/ST entrepreneurs

Employment Generation:
Each project should generate:
- At least 1-2 jobs in service sector
- At least 2-3 jobs in manufacturing sector

Training Support:

Training Institutes:

After Training:

Special Features:

For Women Entrepreneurs:

Common Reasons for Rejection:
- Incomplete application
- Invalid documents
- Over age limit
- Under-qualified
- Existing loan defaults
- Non-eligible activity
- Insufficient market potential
- Poor project viability

Tips for Successful Application:

1. Project Selection:
   - Choose viable business idea
   - Assess local demand
   - Check competition
   - Ensure availability of raw materials

2. Project Report:
   - Detailed and realistic
   - Include market survey
   - Show employment generation
   - Proper cost estimates

3. Documentation:
   - Complete and authentic
   - Properly attested
   - Recent documents (not older than 6 months)

4. Training:
   - Complete EDP training before applying
   - Improves selection chances
   - Better preparation

5. Bank Selection:
   - Choose bank familiar with PMEGP
   - Good track record in MSME lending
   - Local branch preferred

FAQs:

Q: Can I apply for PMEGP if I have GST registration?
A: Yes, GST registration is not a barrier. New ventures can apply.

Q: Is PMEGP subsidy taxable?
A: No, subsidy received under PMEGP is not taxable income.

Q: Can I relocate my unit after setup?
A: Not recommended. Prior permission from KVIC required.

Q: Can I change my project after approval?
A: Major changes not allowed. Minor modifications with KVIC approval.

Q: What if my loan application is rejected by bank?
A: You can approach another bank through KVIC office.

Q: Can I get PMEGP for second venture?
A: No, PMEGP assistance is provided only once per person.

Q: Is collateral required?
A: No collateral for projects up to ₹10 lakhs under CGTMSE.

Q: Can family members apply separately?
A: Yes, but for different activities/locations.
