CGTMSE - Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises

Complete Guide to Collateral-Free Loans for MSMEs

What is CGTMSE?
CGTMSE is a scheme launched by Government of India to provide collateral-free credit to micro and small enterprises. It was set up jointly by Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises and SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India).

Establishment: August 2000
Objective: Enable banks to provide collateral-free loans to MSMEs

Key Features:

Guarantee Coverage:
- Loans up to ₹5 crore
- No collateral or third-party guarantee required
- Credit guarantee to lending institutions
- Reduces risk for banks, encourages lending

Guarantee Amount:
For loans up to ₹5 lakh: 85% guarantee coverage
For loans from ₹5 lakh to ₹5 crore: 75% guarantee coverage
For loans to women entrepreneurs: 80% guarantee coverage (special window)

Eligible Borrowers:

Type of Enterprises:
- New micro and small enterprises (MSEs)
- Existing MSEs requiring additional credit
- Individual proprietorships
- Partnership firms

Sector Coverage:
- Manufacturing units

Enterprise Classification:

Small Enterprise:
- Investment in plant & machinery: Up to ₹10 crore

How CGTMSE Works:

Step 1: Borrower Applies to Bank
- MSE approaches bank for loan
- Bank evaluates as per normal lending norms
- No collateral/third-party guarantee required

Step 2: Bank Sanctions Loan
- If viable, bank sanctions loan
- Loan agreement executed
- No security/collateral taken

Step 3: Bank Applies for Guarantee
- Bank submits guarantee application to CGTMSE
- Online application through CGTMSE portal
- Guarantee fee payment

Step 4: Guarantee Approval
- CGTMSE reviews and approves
- Guarantee certificate issued
- Coverage becomes effective

Step 5: Loan Disbursement
- Bank disburses loan to borrower
- Guarantee cover in place
- Loan monitoring begins

Guarantee Fees:

One-Time Fee Structure:

For loans up to ₹5 lakh:
- Annual guarantee fee: 1.5% of sanctioned amount
- Payable upfront for entire loan tenure

For loans ₹5 lakh to ₹2 crore:
- Annual guarantee fee: 0.75% + 0.75% = 1.5%
- First year: 0.75% paid by borrower
- Subsequent years: 0.75% paid by lending institution

For loans ₹2 crore to ₹5 crore:
- Annual guarantee fee: 0.75% + 0.85% = 1.6%
- First year: 0.75% paid by borrower
- Subsequent years: 0.85% paid by lending institution

Women Entrepreneurs:
- 50% concession in guarantee fee
- Effective fee: 0.375% instead of 0.75%

Special Categories (Micro enterprises in North-East):
- Additional fee concessions available

Service Charge:
- One-time service charge: ₹500 to ₹3000
- Depends on loan amount

Claim Settlement Process:

When Can Bank Claim?
- If borrower defaults on loan
- After all recovery efforts exhausted
- Account classified as NPA (Non-Performing Asset)
- After 18 months from date of first default

Claim Settlement:
- Bank submits claim to CGTMSE
- Required documentation provided
- CGTMSE reviews claim
- If valid, settles 75-85% of outstanding
- Bank pursues recovery from borrower
- Recovered amount shared with CGTMSE

Documentation Required:

From Borrower (at Application):
- Project report/business plan
- KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Address proof
- Bank statements (6 months)
- Business registration proof
- Income tax returns (2 years)
- Udyam registration certificate

- Partnership deed/MOA-AOA (if applicable)

From Bank (for Guarantee):

- Loan agreement copy
- Board resolution/delegation of powers
- Borrower's undertaking
- Guarantee fee payment proof
- Online application form

Common Queries:

Q: Is guarantee fee refundable?
A: No, guarantee fee is non-refundable.

Q: Can I prepay loan?
A: Yes, prepayment allowed as per bank's policy.

Q: What if my loan is rejected?
A: You can reapply after addressing concerns or try another bank.

Q: Is CGTMSE available for all banks?
A: Only for registered Member Lending Institutions.

Q: Can I get guarantee for working capital?
A: Yes, working capital loans are covered.

Q: What if I already have existing loans?
A: Existing secured loans must be replaced/closed for CGTMSE coverage.

Q: Is personal guarantee taken?
A: Yes, personal guarantee of promoters/directors is taken.

Q: Can partnership firms apply?
A: Yes, all types of business entities are eligible.
