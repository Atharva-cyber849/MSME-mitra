Stand-Up India Scheme - Complete Guide

Empowering SC/ST and Women Entrepreneurs

What is Stand-Up India?
Stand-Up India Scheme aims to facilitate bank loans between ₹10 lakhs to ₹1 crore to at least one Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch for setting up a greenfield enterprise.

Launch Date: April 5, 2016
Extended Until: 2025 (further extensions expected)
Nodal Agency: SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India)

Scheme Objectives:
- Promote entrepreneurship among SC/ST and women
- Facilitate bank loans for greenfield enterprises
- Create employment opportunities
- Achieve financial inclusion
- Support economic development in underserved segments

Key Features:

Loan Amount:
- Minimum: ₹10 lakhs
- Maximum: ₹1 crore
- For manufacturing, services, or trading sector
- Composite loan (term loan + working capital)

Loan Distribution:
- 75% for term loan (machinery, equipment, infrastructure)
- 25% for working capital
- Adjustable based on business requirements

Interest Rate:
- Lowest applicable rate for that category
- Base Rate + 3% spread + tenor premium
- Typically 10-14% per annum

Margin Money:
- Maximum 15% of project cost
- Minimum 10% of project cost
- Can be from own sources or government schemes

Eligible Borrowers:

Primary Eligibility:
1. SC/ST entrepreneurs (at least 1 per branch)
2. Women entrepreneurs (at least 1 per branch)
3. Age: Above 18 years
4. First-time borrower from bank for business purpose

Business Criteria:
- Greenfield project only (new enterprise)
- Manufacturing, services, or trading sector
- Non-farm income generating activity
- Proprietorship, partnership, private limited company, or LLP

Greenfield Enterprise Definition:
A greenfield enterprise is one that:
- Is being set up for the first time
- Has not been financed by any bank or financial institution
- Is not an existing/already functioning enterprise
- Has not undergone any rehabilitation/restructuring

Not Eligible:
- Existing enterprises
- Refinancing of existing debt
- Purchase of land (but can lease)
- Speculative business
- Agricultural activities (farming, plantation)

Loan Components:

Term Loan (75%):
For acquiring:
- Machinery and equipment
- Building/shed construction or renovation
- Infrastructure development
- Furniture and fixtures
- Pre-operative expenses
- Technical know-how
- Initial deposits (electricity, water)

Working Capital (25%):
For funding:
- Raw material inventory
- Work-in-progress
- Finished goods stock
- Receivables
- Operating expenses (first few months)

Project Eligibility:

Eligible Sectors:
- Manufacturing: Any legal manufacturing activity
- Services: Healthcare, education, transportation, hospitality, professional services
- Trading: Wholesale, retail, e-commerce

Preferred Activities:
- Food processing units
- Textile and garment manufacturing
- Handicrafts and cottage industries
- Beauty and wellness services
- Healthcare and diagnostic centers
- Educational institutions
- IT and ITES services
- Logistics and warehousing
- E-commerce and digital services

Excluded Activities:
- Speculative activities
- Agricultural/horticultural operations
- Real estate business
- Manufacturing/trading of prohibited goods
- Activities not permissible under law

Application Process:

Step 1: Registration on Stand-Up India Portal
- Visit: www.standupmitra.in
- Click "Apply Online"
- Create account with email and mobile
- Verify through OTP

Step 2: Complete Online Application
Fill details:
- Personal information (name, DOB, address, category)
- Educational and skill background
- Project details (type, location, cost)
- Financial requirements
- Employment generation plan
- Upload documents

Step 3: Document Submission
Required Documents:
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID)
- Address proof (recent)
- Caste certificate (SC/ST - issued by competent authority)
- Educational certificates
- ITR/Form 16 (last 2 years)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Project report (detailed)
- Quotations for machinery/equipment
- Business registration proof
- Land/premises document (owned or lease agreement)
- Partnership deed/MOA-AOA (if applicable)
- Photographs

Step 4: Project Report Preparation
Must include:
- Executive summary
- Promoter background and experience
- Project description
- Market analysis and demand assessment
- Competition analysis
- Production/service delivery plan
- Infrastructure requirements
- Manpower requirements
- Cost of project (detailed breakup)
- Means of finance
- Projected financials (3-5 years):
  * Profit & Loss statement
  * Balance sheet
  * Cash flow statement
- Break-even analysis
- Loan repayment schedule
- Risk analysis and mitigation

Step 5: Bank Selection and Submission
- Choose your bank branch
- Application forwarded electronically
- Can also apply directly at branch
- Bank receives complete documentation

Step 6: Bank Processing
- Initial scrutiny
- Technical evaluation
- Financial viability assessment
- Field inspection
- Personal discussion with borrower
- Credit appraisal
- Sanction committee review

Step 7: Loan Sanction
- Sanction letter issued
- Terms and conditions specified
- Documentation and legal formalities
- Execution of loan documents

Step 8: Disbursement
- In stages as per project progress
- First installment: After completion of documentation
- Subsequent installments: Based on project implementation
- Final installment: After unit commissioning

Special Support Features:

1. Handholding Support:
- Convergence platform for mentoring
- Industry experts guidance
- Business advisors
- Marketing support
- Technology assistance

2. Credit Guarantee:
- CGFMU (Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units)
- 80% guarantee coverage on loan
- Reduced collateral requirements
- Makes lending easier

3. Training and Skill Development:
- Pre-loan training programs
- Entrepreneurship Development Programs (EDP)
- Sector-specific skill training
- Digital literacy programs
- Financial literacy training

4. Convergence with Government Schemes:
- Can combine with PMEGP subsidy
- Can utilize CGTMSE benefits
- Integration with MUDRA scheme
- Linkage with state subsidy schemes

Repayment Terms:

Manufacturing Units:
- Moratorium period: 18 months (max)
- Repayment tenure: 7 years (max)
- Monthly/Quarterly installments

Service Units:
- Moratorium period: 12 months (max)
- Repayment tenure: 7 years (max)
- Monthly/Quarterly installments

Trading Units:
- Moratorium period: 6-12 months
- Repayment tenure: 5-7 years
- Monthly installments

Prepayment:
- Allowed without penalty
- Can prepay partially or fully
- Helps reduce interest burden

Challenge 3: Marketing Difficulties
Solution: Platform for market linkages, GeM registration

Challenge 4: Working Capital Constraints
Solution: 25% working capital component in loan

Challenge 5: Technology Adoption
Solution: Digital training and technology advisory
