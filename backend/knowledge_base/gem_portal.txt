Complete Guide to Selling on GeM for MSMEs

What is GeM?
Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is a one-stop online portal for procurement of goods and services by all Central and State Government Ministries/Departments, PSUs, and other autonomous bodies.

Launch Date: August 9, 2016
Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Commerce and Industry (Department of Commerce)

Portal URL: https://gem.gov.in

Seller Registration Process:

Step 1: Visit GeM Portal
- Go to https://gem.gov.in
- Click on "Seller Registration"
- Choose entity type

Step 2: Select Entity Type
Options:
- Proprietary Firm
- Partnership Firm
- Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- Private Limited Company
- Public Limited Company
- Cooperative Society
- Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
- Others (Trust, Society)

Step 3: Fill Registration Form
Required Information:
- PAN (mandatory)
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Business name
- Address
- Banking details
- GSTIN (if applicable)
- Udyam/MSME registration

Step 4: Upload Documents
Essential Documents:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card (for proprietorship)
- Cancelled cheque/bank statement
- GST certificate
- Udyam registration certificate
- Address proof (electricity bill/rent agreement)
- Partnership deed/MOA-AOA/Registration certificate
- Board resolution (for companies)
- Photograph (passport size)

Optional Documents (depending on category):
- BIS certification
- ISO certification
- Make in India certificate
- Startup recognition
- Manufacturing license
- Quality certificates

Step 5: Verification
- OTP verification on mobile and email
- Documents verified by GeM team
- Usually completed within 3-5 working days
- Profile activated after approval

Step 6: Complete Seller Profile
- Upload company logo
- Add business description
- Set payment preferences
- Complete cataloging training

Listing Products on GeM:

Step 1: Select Category
- Login to seller account
- Go to "Add Product"
- Select appropriate category
- Choose sub-category

Step 2: Provide Product Details
Mandatory Information:
- Product name (as per category)
- Brand name
- Model number
- Manufacturer details
- Country of origin
- HSN code
- Technical specifications (as per template)
- Product images (high quality, multiple angles)
- Product description
- Warranty/guarantee details
- Delivery timeline

Step 3: Pricing
- Enter MRP
- Enter offered price on GeM
- GST will be added automatically
- Consider shipping costs
- Set minimum order quantity (MOQ)
- Maximum order quantity

Step 4: Upload Certificates
Quality Certificates:
- BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) - mandatory for many products
- ISI mark
- ISO 9001 (Quality Management)
- ISO 14001 (Environmental Management)
- CE marking (for electrical/electronic)
- FSSAI (for food products)

Other Certifications:
- Test reports
- Performance certificates
- Energy star ratings
- Make in India certificate

Step 5: Set Location
- Specify state(s) where you can supply
- Pan-India or state-specific
- Affects delivery timeline
- Pincode servicing areas

Step 6: Submit for Approval
- Review all details
- Submit for GeM verification
- Usually approved within 24-48 hours
- Live on GeM after approval

Types of Selling on GeM:

1. Direct Purchase (L1 Seller):
- Buyer buys directly from lowest priced seller (L1)
- Instant order
- No bidding required
- Fastest transaction

2. Bidding (RA - Reverse Auction):
- Buyer floats bid
- Sellers quote prices
- Lowest bidder wins
- Competitive pricing

3. Demand Aggregation:
- Multiple buyers pool requirements
- Single seller supplies all
- Volume discount opportunity
- State/Central aggregation

Pricing Strategy:

Competitive Pricing:
- Check competitor prices on GeM
- Consider total cost (product + delivery)
- Factor in GST
- Account for payment cycle
- Keep margins reasonable

Dynamic Pricing:
- Update prices regularly
- Respond to market changes
- Special discounts for bulk orders
- Festival/year-end offers

Price Comparison:

- Quality and service also matter
- Certifications add value

Payment Terms:

Payment Timeline:
- 10 days from delivery (standard)
- Auto-payment through PFMS
- Direct to seller's bank account
- No payment delays

Payment Method:
- Electronic transfer only
- Through Public Financial Management System (PFMS)
- Transparent and auditable
- Linked to Aadhaar (for proprietors)

Advance Payment:
- 10-30% advance for certain categories
- Helps with working capital
- Milestone-based payments for large orders

Packing & Dispatch:

Delivery:

Invoice & Payment:

Common Challenges & Solutions:

Challenge 1: High Competition
Solution: Focus on quality, certifications, customer service, competitive pricing

Challenge 2: Payment Delays (rare)
Solution: Ensure complete documentation, track on PFMS portal, escalate if needed

Challenge 3: Specifications Mismatch
Solution: Read requirements carefully, clarify doubts before bidding, meet exact specs

Challenge 4: Logistics Issues
Solution: Partner with reliable logistics, track shipments, update buyers proactively

Challenge 5: Return/Rejection
Solution: Maintain quality, proper packaging, meet specifications, pre-dispatch check
